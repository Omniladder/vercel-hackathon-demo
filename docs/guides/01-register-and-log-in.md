# 01: Register and log in

> **Status:** Built.

Use this guide to make an account and sign in. You need an email address and a password.

The login page has one button, **Log in**. It is used for both jobs. If the email already has an account, the app logs you in. If the email is new, the app creates the account and logs you in.

## Log in or create an account

1. Open the app. The login page appears.
2. Type your email address in the **Email** box.
3. Type your password in the **Password** box.
4. Click **Log in**.
5. The dashboard opens. If the email was new, the account was just created.

If the app asks you to confirm your email, open the message from Supabase, click the link, then come back and follow the steps again.

## When something goes wrong

- **"Enter your email and password."** One of the boxes is empty. Fill in both.
- **"That password is wrong for this email."** The email already has an account, and the password does not match it. Try the password again.
- **"Check your email to confirm your account, then log in."** The account was created, but you need to confirm your email first. Open the message from Supabase, click the link, then log in.
- **Any other message in red under the form.** The message comes from the sign-in service, for example "Email not confirmed". Read it, fix what it asks for, and try again.
- **The buttons are greyed out for a moment.** The app is still working. Wait for it to finish.

## Stay signed in

The app keeps you signed in between visits on the same browser. If you open the login page while you are signed in, it sends you straight to the dashboard. To sign out, see [05: Log out](05-log-out.md).
