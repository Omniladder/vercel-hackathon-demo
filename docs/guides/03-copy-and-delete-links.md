# 03: Copy and delete links

> **Status:** Built.

The dashboard lists every short link you have created, with the newest one at the top. The table has three columns: **Name**, **Redirects to** (the original address), and **Short link**. Each row has four buttons: **Visit**, **Copy URL**, **Copy short link**, and **Delete**.

## Copy a short link

1. On the dashboard, find the row for the link.
2. Click **Copy short link** to copy the short link, or **Copy URL** to copy the original address.
3. The button says **Copied!** for a moment.
4. Paste the link wherever you need it, such as a chat or a slide.

If your browser blocks copying, the app shows an alert: "Could not copy. Select this text and copy it yourself:", followed by the text. Select the text in the alert and copy it yourself.

## Delete a short link

1. On the dashboard, find the row for the link.
2. Click **Delete**.
3. Your browser asks: "Delete this short link? Anyone who opens it will see "Link not found"." Click **OK** to delete the link, or **Cancel** to keep it. The button names come from your browser.

After you click **OK**, the page reloads and the row is gone. The short link stops working, so anyone who opens it will see **Link not found**.

If the delete fails, your browser shows an alert with the error message.

You can only delete your own links. The app does not show links that belong to other people.

## When the list is empty

If you have no links yet, the dashboard shows **Welcome!** and the message "You don't have any short links yet. Click the button to make your first shortened URL." Click **+ Create new** to make your first one. See [02: Create a short link](02-create-a-short-link.md).

## When the list does not load

If the list fails to load, the app does not show an error message. The dashboard may show the **Welcome!** message even though you have links. Reload the page. If your links still do not appear, see [06: Troubleshooting](06-troubleshooting.md).
