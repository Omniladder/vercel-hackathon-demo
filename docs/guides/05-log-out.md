# 05: Log out

> **Status:** Built.

Log out when you finish using the app, especially on a shared computer.

## Steps

1. On the dashboard, click **Log out**. It is in the top right of the page.
2. The login page appears.

Logging out signs you out only in this browser. If you are signed in on another device, you stay signed in there.

## After you log out

- Your short links are still saved. Log in again to see them.
- Short links you shared still work for anyone who has them. Logging out does not delete them.
- If you press the Back button, the browser may show the dashboard again. It shows no links, because you are signed out. Log in again to see your links.
