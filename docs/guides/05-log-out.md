# 05: Log out

> **Status:** Not built yet. The steps describe the planned header and logout button.

Log out when you finish using the app, especially on a shared computer.

## Steps

1. Click **Log out** in the top bar of the app.
2. The login page appears.

## After you log out

- Pressing the Back button does not show the dashboard again. The app checks your session first.
- Your short links are still saved. Log in again to see them.
- Short links you shared still work for anyone who has them. Logging out does not delete them.
